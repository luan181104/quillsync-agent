# LG webOS Smart TVs (Consumer Grade)

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/56946344474003-LG-webOS-Smart-TVs-Consumer-Grade

### In this article, we'll install the OptiSigns app on an LG webOS Smart TV and pair it with your OptiSigns account.

- [Before You Start](#BeforeYouStart)
- [Install the OptiSigns App](#InstallTheOptiSignsApp)
- [Pair the TV with Your Account](#PairTheTVWithYourAccount)
- [Open OptiSigns Each Day](#OpenOptiSignsEachDay)
- [Next Steps](#NextSteps)

You can run OptiSigns on an LG webOS Smart TV, so your signage plays straight from the TV without any extra hardware.

| **IMPORTANT** |
| --- |
| For most signage, we recommend a dedicated player such as the [OptiSigns Android Player](https://shop.optisigns.com/products/optisigns-android-stick-player-2) instead. LG webOS Smart TVs don't let apps start automatically, so someone has to open OptiSigns each time the TV turns on. The app also doesn't save your content on the TV. It plays content over the internet, so it needs a steady connection, and the screen can't keep playing if the network drops. An LG webOS TV works well for a small playlist when the connection is reliable and someone can open the app each day. |

This article is for consumer LG Smart TVs. If you have a commercial LG display that runs webOS Signage, see our [LG webOS Signage article](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/4414496579475) instead.

---

## Before You Start

- Connect the TV to the internet. A wired connection is best, because OptiSigns streams your content.
- Update the TV's software from its **Settings** menu.

---

## Install the OptiSigns App

Press the **Home** button on the remote and open the **LG Content Store**. Search for **OptiSigns**.

Select the OptiSigns app, then select **Install**.

When it finishes, open OptiSigns from the home screen.

The app shows a pairing code. Keep it on screen; you'll need it to pair.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| If you can't find OptiSigns in the LG Content Store, it isn't available for your TV model or region. Consider a dedicated player such as the [OptiSigns Android Player](https://shop.optisigns.com/products/optisigns-android-stick-player-2). |

---

## Pair the TV with Your Account

1. On a computer or phone, go to [app.optisigns.com](https://app.optisigns.com/) and log in. If you don't have an account yet, select **Create Account**. You can also log in with Google, Microsoft or Facebook.

![OptiSigns portal log-in page with Google, Microsoft, Facebook and SSO options](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56946372570515)

2. On the **Screens** page, select **Add Screen**.

![Screens page toolbar with an arrow on the Add Screen button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56946344982547)

3. Enter the pairing code shown on your TV in **Pair code**, then select **Pair**.

![Add Screen dialog with a pair code entered and the Pair button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56946363898259)

**Note:** Codes are case\-sensitive, and the portal enters them in capitals. If the code on your TV has lowercase letters, select **Use lowercase pairing code** and type it exactly as shown.

---

## Open OptiSigns on Startup

Since LG webOS TVs don't start apps automatically, open OptiSigns from the home screen each time the TV turns on. Once it's open, it plays whatever you've assigned to the screen in the portal.
