# Present or Share Your Screen with OptiSigns and the AeriCast Add-on

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/14502378549907-Present-or-Share-Your-Screen-with-OptiSigns-and-the-AeriCast-Add-on

### In this article, we'll show how to present from a laptop or from an iPhone, iPad or Android phone or tablet to a screen running OptiSigns with the AeriCast add\-on.

- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed)
- [Find the Presenter Code](#FindThePresenterCode)
- [Present from a Laptop](#PresentFromALaptop)
	- [Presenting with Colleagues](#PresentingWithColleagues)
- [Present from a Phone or Tablet](#PresentFromAPhoneOrTablet)
- [Troubleshooting](#Troubleshooting)

Share your laptop, phone or tablet to any screen running OptiSigns with the AeriCast Wireless Presentation app. There are no cables to connect: enter the code shown on the screen and start presenting.

---

## What You'll Need

- A screen running OptiSigns with the AeriCast Wireless Presentation app. To set it up, see [How to Use AeriCast Add\-on for Wireless Presentation and Video Conferencing](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/14502723487379).
- To present from a laptop: Chrome or Firefox
- To present from a phone or tablet: the free AeriCast Presenter app

## Present from a Laptop

On your laptop, go to [present.aericast.com](https://present.aericast.com/). Chrome or Firefox give the best experience.

Enter the presenter code and click **Continue**.

![AeriCast Wireless Presentation page in a browser with the presenter code entered and the Continue button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182964726291)

You're now connected to the room. Click **Present** to start sharing.

![AeriCast page showing the room name and presenter code with the Present button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182964726931)

Choose what to share:

- **Entire Screen** \- Your whole screen. Select the screen, then click **Share**.
- **Window** \- One application.
- **Chrome Tab** \- One tab in your browser.

![Browser share dialog with the entire screen selected and the Share button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182964727827)

To stop presenting, click **Stop** on the AeriCast page, or **Stop sharing** in the browser's sharing bar.

![AeriCast page while sharing with arrows on the Stop button and the browser's Stop sharing button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182968967443)

### Presenting with Colleagues

To let a colleague present in the same room, share the presenter code with them.

![AeriCast page with an arrow on the presenter code to share with other presenters](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182968969107)

When more than one person joins, AeriCast lists the presenters. When it's your turn, stop the current presenter and share your screen.

![AeriCast page listing two presenters while one is sharing their screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182968970387)

## Present from a Phone or Tablet

Download the AeriCast Presenter app:

- iPhone and iPad: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aericast-presenter/id1629924738)
- Android: [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aericast.presenter)

![AeriCast Presenter logo with Google Play and App Store badges and a QR code to download the app](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182968976915)

Open the AeriCast Presenter app, enter the presenter code shown on your screen, and tap **Continue**.

![AeriCast Presenter app with a presenter code entered and the Continue button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182964741651)

Tap the button above **Tap to present**.

![AeriCast Presenter app showing the presenter code with an arrow on the Tap to present button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182968981139)

Tap **Start Broadcast**. Your phone or tablet now shows on the screen.

![Phone screen broadcast dialog with AeriCast Presenter selected and an arrow on Start Broadcast](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182968982291)

![Phone screen broadcast dialog while broadcasting, with the Stop Broadcast option](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182968983443)

When you're done, tap the red recording indicator in the top corner of your phone, then tap **Stop**.

![Phone Screen Broadcasting prompt with arrows on the red recording indicator and the Stop button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57182968986387)

## Troubleshooting

Here are some of the common issues people have with using Aericast on OptiSigns, and their solutions.

#### There's no presenter code on the screen

The AeriCast Wireless Presentation app isn't playing on that screen. Assign it to the screen, or add it to the screen's playlist. See [How to Use AeriCast Add\-on for Wireless Presentation and Video Conferencing](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/14502723487379).

#### The Share dialog doesn't appear in my browser

Use Chrome or Firefox, and allow present.aericast.com to share your screen if the browser asks.
