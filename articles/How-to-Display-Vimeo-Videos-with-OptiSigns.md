# How to Display Vimeo Videos with OptiSigns

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016254994-How-to-Display-Vimeo-Videos-with-OptiSigns

We support Vimeo out of the box.

Log on to OptiSigns and use the Vimeo App to create an asset.

![Add App dialog with Vimeo typed in the search box and the Vimeo app tile highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56349070687379)

Then go to Vimeo and find the URL of the video you wish to display:

![vimeo url example](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56349070687891)

Then Paste the link to OptiSigns' Vimeo app:

![Vimeo app form with a name entered and a Vimeo video URL pasted in the highlighted URL field](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56349117837331)

You then can use this Vimeo video on your playlist or assign directly to screen if you'd like.

![Files/Assets grid showing the new Vimeo asset with its video thumbnail as the first card](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56349117838227)

 

**Note**: If you want to set your Vimeo as not public, you can set your Viemo as "**Hide from Vimeo".** Then you can add "[social\-player.optisigns.com](http://social-player.optisigns.com/)" in the Specific domains of Embed.

![Vimeo privacy settings: Hide from Vimeo selected, social-player.optisigns.com added under Specific domains](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/15320186326547)
