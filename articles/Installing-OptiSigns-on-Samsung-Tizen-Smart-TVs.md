# Installing OptiSigns on Samsung Tizen Smart TVs

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/14726570281875-Installing-OptiSigns-on-Samsung-Tizen-Smart-TVs

### In this article, we'll install the OptiSigns app on a Samsung Tizen Smart TV and pair it with your OptiSigns account.

- [Before You Start](#BeforeYouStart)
- [Install the OptiSigns App](#InstallTheOptiSignsApp)
- [Pair the TV with Your Account](#PairTheTVWithYourAccount)
- [Open OptiSigns Each Day](#OpenOptiSignsEachDay)
- [Use the OptiSigns Menu](#UseTheOptiSignsMenu)
- [Next Steps](#NextSteps)

The OptiSigns app is available in the Samsung Smart TV app store, so you can run your signage straight from a Samsung Tizen TV without any extra hardware.

| **IMPORTANT** |
| --- |
| For most signage, we recommend a dedicated player such as the [OptiSigns Android Player](https://shop.optisigns.com/products/optisigns-android-stick-player-2) instead. Samsung Tizen TVs don't let apps start automatically, so someone has to open OptiSigns each time the TV turns on. They also have very little free storage, usually only a few hundred MB. OptiSigns saves your content on the TV so it keeps playing if the network drops, but with that little space, only a small playlist fits. A Tizen TV works well for a small playlist of a few images, videos or apps when someone can open the app each day. |

---

## Before You Start

Connect the TV to the internet, and update the TV's software. Go to **Settings** \> **Support** \> **Software Update**.

---

## Install the OptiSigns App

Press the **Home** button on the remote and open the app store (**Apps**, or **Smart Hub** on older models).

Search for **OptiSigns**.

Select the OptiSigns app, then select **Install**. Installing can take a few minutes, depending on your internet connection.

When it finishes, select **Open**. You can also open OptiSigns later from **Apps** on the home screen.

The app shows a pairing code. Keep it on screen; you'll need it to pair.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| OptiSigns isn't available for every Samsung model or region. If you're in the US and can't find OptiSigns in the app store, your TV model most likely isn't supported. Consider a dedicated player such as the [OptiStick Player](https://shop.optisigns.com/products/optisigns-android-stick-player-2). |

---

## Pair the TV with Your Account

On a computer or phone, go to [app.optisigns.com](https://app.optisigns.com/) and log in. If you don't have an account yet, select **Create Account**. You can also log in with Google, Microsoft or Facebook.

![OptiSigns portal log-in page with Google, Microsoft, Facebook and SSO options](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56957620078483)

On the **Screens** page, select **Add Screen**.

![Screens page toolbar with an arrow on the Add Screen button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56957613225107)

Enter the pairing code shown on your TV in **Pair code**, then select **Pair**.

![Add Screen dialog with a pair code entered and the Pair button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56957620080147)

**Note:** Codes are case\-sensitive, and the portal enters them in capitals. If the code on your TV has lowercase letters, select **Use lowercase pairing code** and type it exactly as shown.

---

## Open OptiSigns Each Day

Because Tizen TVs don't start apps automatically, open OptiSigns from **Apps** each time the TV turns on. Once it's open, it plays whatever you've assigned to the screen in the portal.

---

## Use the OptiSigns Menu

Press **OK** on the remote to open the OptiSigns menu. Use the arrow buttons to move through it, and press **Back** to close it.

The menu includes:

- **Refresh:** reload the content assigned to the screen.
- **Orientation:** show the current orientation.
- **Mute:** turn the sound off or on.
- **Advanced Options:** more settings, including **Video Player**, **Show Downloading Status** and **Show Offline Indicator**.
- **TroubleShooting** and **About:** details that help our support team if something isn't working.
- **Unpair** and **Exit**.
