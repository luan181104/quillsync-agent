# How to Fix the Scaling Issue on OptiSigns Android Devices

Article URL: https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/28026588373139-How-to-Fix-the-Scaling-Issue-on-OptiSigns-Android-Devices

### In this article, we'll show how to change the display size on your OptiSigns Android Stick so text and graphics show at the right scale, on every device generation.

- [What Scaling Looks Like](#WhatScalingLooksLike)
- [What You'll Need](#WhatYoullNeed)
- [Change the Display Size on the Android Stick Gen 3](#ChangeTheDisplaySizeOnTheAndroidStickGen3)
- [Change the Display Size on Earlier Android Sticks](#ChangeTheDisplaySizeOnEarlierAndroidSticks)
- [After You Change the Setting](#AfterYouChangeTheSetting)

If text, charts or web pages look too large or too small on your OptiSigns Android Stick, the device's display size setting is the cause. You can change it from the device's own Settings menu in a minute.

---

## What Scaling Looks Like

The display size controls how many dots per inch (dpi) Android draws with. A lower setting fits more on the screen; a higher one makes everything bigger. Here is the same web page at two settings.

At 160 dpi, the page is small and fits more on screen:

![Yahoo News web page shown small at 160 dpi, with lots of empty space around the article](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234513862931)

At 480 dpi, the same page is large and shows less:

![The same Yahoo News web page shown large at 480 dpi, with the headline filling the screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234559690771)

---

## What You'll Need

- An OptiSigns Android Stick connected to your screen
- The remote that came with it, or a USB mouse

The steps depend on your device's generation. If the home screen shows an **OptiSigns** tile, a large **Settings** tile and **Mix Apps**, you have a Gen 3 device. If it shows an **Apps** button, a row of app icons and a settings gear in the top\-right corner, follow the steps for earlier devices.

---

## Change the Display Size on the Android Stick Gen 3

On the home screen, select **Settings**.

![OptiSigns Gen 3 home screen with an arrow pointing to the Settings tile](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234513867027)

Select **Device Preferences**.

![Settings menu with Device Preferences highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234513870483)

Select **Display \& Sound**.

![Device Preferences menu with Display & Sound highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234513871635)

Select **Display Size**.

![Display & Sound menu with Display Size highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234559698067)

On the **Display Size** page, press left or right on your remote to make everything on the screen smaller or larger.

![Display Size page with a slider, the minus button marked 1 and the plus button marked 2](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234559699091)

---

## Change the Display Size on Earlier Android Sticks

On the home screen, select the settings gear in the top\-right corner.

![Android Stick home screen with an arrow pointing to the settings gear at the top right](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234513877907)

Select **Android settings**.

![Settings menu with Android settings highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234513881107)

Select **Extra settings**.

![Android settings menu with Extra settings highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234513883155)

Select **Display**.

![Extra settings menu with Display highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234559705619)

Select **Display size**. It shows the current setting, for example **Default 320dpi**.

![Display settings with Display size highlighted, showing Default 320dpi](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234559706131)

Press right on your remote until the **\-** button is highlighted, then use it and the **\+** button to change the dpi.

![Display size slider set to Default 320dpi with the minus button marked 1](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/57234513891731)

---

## After You Change the Setting

Open the OptiSigns app again. Your content now shows at the new scale. If it still isn't right, repeat the steps and try one step smaller or larger.

| **TIP** |
| --- |
| If the edges of your content are cut off rather than too big or too small, the TV is zooming the picture (overscan). Look in your TV's picture settings for a zoom, aspect or overscan option. For other players, see [My Image Is Bleeding off the Screen](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041570153). |
