The OptiSigns channel turns a Roku device or Roku TV into a digital signage player. It works best for static menus and simple slideshows.

Roku has a lot of platform restrictions. For better compatibility and reliability, we recommend a dedicated player such as the [OptiSigns Android Stick Player](https://links.optisigns.com/szzk), or a [Windows, Mac or Linux](https://www.optisigns.com/download) device.

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| Since the Roku OS 12\.0\.0 update, Roku is stricter about power management and screensavers. |

---

## Choose How to Run OptiSigns on Roku

You can run OptiSigns on Roku in two ways:

- **Channel/app:** plays images, videos and documents, but doesn't start by itself. This article covers the channel.
- **Screensaver:** starts by itself when the Roku is idle, but plays images only. Videos are skipped. See [How to Install OptiSigns as Screensaver on Roku](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500002383502).

---

## What the Roku Channel Supports

- Images (except GIF and SVG), videos and documents. Documents play one page at a time.
- Canvas apps.
- Playlists and schedules.
- Landscape and portrait, with rotation of 90, 180 or 270 degrees.

---

## What the Roku Channel Doesn't Support

- Most other apps, such as social media, YouTube, news and streaming video.
- Split Screen.
- Large content libraries. Roku has little storage, so keep the content you assign to it small.

For the full list, see [Roku Release Notes \& Limitations](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042143333).

## Prepare Your Roku

Before you install the channel, change these settings on your Roku:

1. Go to **Settings** \> **System** \> **Power** \> **Auto Power Saving** and clear **Reduce power after 15 minutes** and **Turn off after 4 hours**.
2. Go to **Settings** \> **Network** \> **Bandwidth saver** and set it to **Off**.

---

## Install the OptiSigns Channel

Add the channel in one of two ways.

### Add It from Your Roku Account

1. Go to [my.roku.com/add/optisigns](https://my.roku.com/add/optisigns) and sign in to your Roku account.
2. Add the channel. Roku adds it to the devices on your account.

### Add It on Your Roku Device

1. On your Roku, open **Search** and type **OptiSigns**.
2. Select **OptiSigns Digital Signage**.

![Roku Search screen with OptiSigns Digital Signage as the top result](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56947727310355)

3. Select **Add channel**.

![OptiSigns Digital Signage channel page on Roku with Add channel selected](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56947727311507)

---

## Open the Channel

Open the **OptiSigns** channel from the Roku Home screen. The first time it opens, it shows a pairing code. Keep it on screen; you'll need it to pair.

![OptiSigns channel on Roku showing a pairing code and setup instructions](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56947704573715)

**Note:** To watch a how\-to video on the pairing screen, press **OK** on the Roku remote. You can also [watch the setup video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=se6WSNu_h0I).

---

## Use the Channel Menu

On the pairing screen, press **Back** or the **\*** (Options) button on the Roku remote to open the menu. It includes:

- **Play:** start playing your assigned content.
- **Refresh:** reload the content from your OptiSigns account.
- **Unpair:** remove the screen from your account and show a new pairing code.
- **Orientation:** choose **Landscape**, **Rotate 90 degrees**, **Rotate 180 degrees** or **Rotate 270 degrees**.
- **Version:** the channel version.

---

## Pair the Player with Your Account

1. Go to [app.optisigns.com](https://app.optisigns.com/) and log in. If you don't have an account yet, select **Create Account**. You can also log in with Google, Microsoft or Facebook.

![OptiSigns portal log-in page with Google, Microsoft, Facebook and SSO options](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56947727313939)

2. On the **Screens** page, select **Add Screen**.

![Screens page toolbar with an arrow on the Add Screen button](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56947704577427)

3. Enter the pairing code shown on the player in **Pair code**, then select **Pair**.

![Add Screen dialog with a pair code entered and the Pair button highlighted](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/article_attachments/56947704579475)

**Note:** Codes are case\-sensitive, and the portal enters them in capitals. If the code on your player has lowercase letters, select **Use lowercase pairing code** and type it exactly as shown. If pairing fails with a case error, the portal switches this on for you, so check the code and select **Pair** again.

The player changes to show **Paired**. When you assign content to the screen, the channel starts playing it.

## Start OptiSigns Automatically

The Roku channel doesn't open by itself when the Roku turns on. To have OptiSigns start on its own, also set it as your Roku screensaver. See [How to Install OptiSigns as Screensaver on Roku](https://support.optisigns.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500002383502).

| **NOTE** |
| --- |
| As a screensaver, OptiSigns plays images only. |

---

## Turn Off the Clicking Sound

If you hear a periodic clicking sound while the channel is running, it's a simulated button press that keeps your display from going idle. To turn it off on the Roku, go to **Settings** \> **Audio** \> **Menu volume** and set it to **Off**.
